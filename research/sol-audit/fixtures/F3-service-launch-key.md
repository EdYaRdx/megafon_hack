At least three materially distinct choices with objections and human gates: status quo/diagnosis; narrow, reversible discovery/probe or paid pilot within 200k/six weeks only if feasible and approved; broader launch only after evidence and required capabilities; stop/close if need, feasibility, or viability fail. A recommendation may favor a bounded test but must define measurable user/business behavior, comparator, duration, threshold, and guardrails or explicitly state inputs needed. No actual launch or external contact is authorized.

- Treating synthetic competitor examples as validated demand or adopting their features as current product capability.
- Proposing campaign automation or claiming benefit from a feature the prototype lacks.
- Ignoring the budget/time limits or making an unapproved external action.
