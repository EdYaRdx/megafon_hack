This is a scenario hurdle, not a forecast or evidence of achievable customers.

- **Unknown:** Reachable audience, recruitment/channel, actual test and build costs, feasible sample size, candidate price, and product delivery beyond return recording.

### Options and recommendation

1. **Proceed with the full six-week validation effort (up to 200,000 ₽).** Could test a narrower returns-recording proposition. **Strongest objection:** feasibility, cost, access, and demand are all unestablished; the cap does not show the work can be delivered or a useful sample reached.
2. **Narrow to learning; keep the prototype unchanged.** First seek a human decision and required permissions for a bounded, record-only value/price test; do not build messaging or claim repeat-customer outcomes. **Strongest objection:** a narrow test may not answer whether a broader service can succeed.
3. **Keep the prototype unchanged with no test.** Preserves the option without spend or unsupported claims. **Strongest objection:** leaves the value and willingness-to-pay questions unresolved.
4. **Stop/close the direction.** Avoids further effort absent evidence. **Strongest objection:** the current evidence does not establish that the narrower recording use case has no value.

**Recommendation:** Option 2, conditional on explicit human approval and feasible recruitment/cost inputs. This is a recommendation to learn, **not** authorization to contact anyone, spend, launch, or publish. It deliberately defers the broader repeat-customer proposition.

### Product, communication, and proposed validation

Keep the product proposition to recording customer returns; do not promise reminders, campaigns, automation, repeat visits, or retention outcomes (constraints.md §2, lines 9–11). If approved, describe only the record-only prototype and its limitations to eligible shop decision-makers; no channel preference or reach is established.

A proposed six-week test, subject to human approval: recruit a feasible, pre-specified sample of independent shops; compare a record-only prototype demonstration/offer against their current return-recording process. The primary metric could be **paid commitments at a human-selected price ÷ eligible shops receiving the offer**, reported by arm with counts and denominator. Set a minimum sample and a go/no-go threshold before recruitment; the packet does not supply a defensible price, sample, or threshold, so these must be supplied and approved rather than inferred. Guardrails: no messaging/automation claims; record withdrawals, complaints, and any data-handling issue; stop on a guardrail breach. Revisit only if the approved threshold is met and costs, permissions, and delivery feasibility are confirmed; otherwise retain the prototype unchanged or stop. This test would not establish retention or broader-market demand.

**Next state:** A human decision-maker must choose the path and, for any real test, authorize contact, recruitment, budget, data handling, and the test’s price/sample/threshold. Until then, no external action is authorized (brief.md lines 13–15; constraints.md §2, lines 7–14).
