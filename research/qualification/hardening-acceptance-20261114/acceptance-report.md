## Confirmed defects and bounded observations

**No product defect is confirmed by these required behavior checks.** The results support the tested instruction-driven workflow. They do not demonstrate sandbox enforcement: stale approval was rejected by the agent, and no host-security bypass was attempted.

One observed boundary deserves independent review: after refusing stale approval, the agent appended an operational review/next action to root state.md and activity while preserving the selected revision, claims and every sealed byte. Root state thus differs byte-for-byte from the selected snapshot. The canonical rule explicitly requires matching root revision/active claims and use of sealed decision snapshots; it does not explicitly require identical root bytes after an operational note. Therefore this is recorded as a **policy interpretation / replay boundary**, not promoted to a confirmed defect solely because a stricter evaluator could demand byte equality. [Exact before/after hashes](receipts/stale-after.json), E-APPROVAL and final fresh resume addendum preserve the evidence for Sol.
