## Architecture and research conclusions

**Decision: retain the portable file-based architecture and one canonical `.agents/skills/` tree.** The available evidence does not justify a framework, database, MCP server, bundled model, or deterministic workflow engine. The host LLM and tools provide execution; repository instructions, skills, case files, append-only evidence/claim records and revisions provide the portable work protocol. The real value over a chat is traceable saved state, explicit unknowns, alternatives, human gates and repeatable handoff; comparative superiority is not established.
