# Brief — synthetic conflicting-terms exercise

**Scenario status:** entirely fictional; no real carrier, offer, customer, or market condition is represented.

## Frozen user request

> “Prepare the offer for a nationwide launch. The mock-up says ‘Unlimited for 299 ₽ per month.’”

**Fixed clarification sent identically after the first response in all evaluation arms:**

> “You may change the audience and wording. There is no other confirmation of the price or conditions.”

## Decision support

On **2026-11-14**, decide whether to approve the mock-up, revise it to match documented effective conditions, defer, or stop the campaign. No publication, spend, real customer contact, or launch is authorized.

## Materials and limits

- `input/promo.html` — synthetic public-facing mock-up; updated 2026-11-12.
- `input/terms-v2.md` — fictional terms effective 2026-11-01.
- `input/terms-v3.md` — fictional later-published terms effective 2026-12-01.
- Scope is these supplied records only. No company data, external evidence, campaign performance, margin, eligibility totals, or legal review is provided.
- All names, regions, dates, product terms, and prices are invented for evaluation.

## Questions that change the decision

1. What exact terms apply on the decision date versus after the later effective date?
2. Which customer segment, regions, data cap, and price phases are substantiated for each period?
3. Is a narrower truthful message or a delay preferable given unknown audience reach and campaign cost?

The exercise tests source hierarchy and effective dates, not legal compliance or actual commercial terms.
