# Validation plan — draft, not approved or launch-ready

- **Learning question:** For a defined bookstore staff task, does a read-only low-stock signal help people identify a genuinely low-stock title and decide what to do, compared with their current process?
- **Hypothesis and falsifier:** `HYPOTHESIS`: eligible staff can correctly identify the configured low-stock condition and a safe next step using the signal. Falsified if participants cannot reliably interpret the signal, it is stale/inaccurate, or it does not improve task performance versus the agreed comparator. No finding yet.
- **Method and comparator:** First, owner-approved discovery of current end-to-end stock-check/replenishment workflow; if justified and separately approved, a moderated task-based usability/feasibility comparison of the read-only display against current workflow. Comparator definition and materials `UNKNOWN`. No supplier/order integration, external action, or live test.
- **Audience/sample and selection limits:** Intended bookstore staff who perform the defined task; sample size, eligible stores/SKUs, recruitment method, accessibility coverage, consent, and selection frame `UNKNOWN`. Existing six stores were selected active dashboard users and are not a representative sample (S-003, `input/pilot-protocol.md:5–11`; E-002). GOV.UK recommends broad user types, including disabled people and people with low digital skills, as method guidance only (S-005/E-005).
- **Primary metric/formula/unit/denominator:** `correctly_completed eligible stock-check tasks / all eligible stock-check tasks attempted`, expressed as a task proportion. Define “correct,” eligibility, exclusions, and handling of missing/abandoned tasks before collection. This measures task performance only, not prevented stockouts.
- **Comparator metric:** Same formula measured on the agreed current workflow/comparator, with identical tasks and eligibility; paired/comparison design, order effects, and analysis plan `UNKNOWN` until owner/research lead specifies them.
- **Observation window:** `UNKNOWN`; owner/research lead must set before any approved collection. The supplied pilot's two-week window is not a justified future test duration.
- **Success/stop threshold:** `UNKNOWN`. Do not invent a percentage or treat 13/18 alert opens as a threshold. Owner must set practical improvement threshold and unacceptable error rate using a baseline and defined stockout/task costs before the test.
- **Guardrail and unwanted effects:** Incorrect/stale low-stock interpretation; false confidence; unsafe or unnecessary ordering; staff burden; accessibility barriers; missing data. Track `incorrect or unsafe decisions / all eligible tasks attempted` and report completion/abandonment and errors with explicit denominator. Acceptable bounds `UNKNOWN`; do not proceed without owner-defined stop criteria.
- **Data source and quality checks:** If approved, protocol-defined task observations and validated stock state; audit eligibility, duplicate/missing records, timestamp/freshness, consistent stockout/low-stock definition, and denominator. No real user or business data currently supplied or authorized.
- **Owner and human approval:** Decision owner and research/data owner `UNKNOWN`. Must be identified and approve recruitment/contact, consent, data use, protocol, comparator, definitions, safeguards, threshold, and timing before any activity.
- **Action for each possible result:** If threshold met and guardrails pass, present evidence/limits to owner for a separate human decision on a reversible read-only next step; no auto-ordering implied. If threshold fails or guardrail breached, stop and investigate workflow/signal or close direction per owner decision. If inconclusive or data quality fails, do not expand/build; revise only with owner approval. No result authorizes external action.
- **Readiness:** NOT READY; comparator, sample, period, definitions, threshold, owners, permissions, and baseline are unresolved.
