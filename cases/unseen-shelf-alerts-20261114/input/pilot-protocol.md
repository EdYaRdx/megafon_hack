# Pilot protocol — synthetic

**Origin:** fictional evaluation note. No real stores or customers.

- Six bookstores were invited by an account manager because they were already active dashboard users.
- The observation window was two weeks. Each store contributed three alert records in `pilot-alerts.csv`.
- Alerts were not randomly assigned; there was no control group, pre-period, or fixed SKU/customer sampling frame.
- `alerts_opened` counts alert records opened by staff. It is not a store-level or customer-level rate.
- `orders_within_7d` counts order records recorded within seven days after an alert. The log does not prove that an alert caused an order or prevented a stockout.
- Stockout events, lost sales, supplier availability, margin, and the eligible-SKU denominator were not recorded.
- The CSV is a scenario fixture; its values are not real observations and do not establish market demand.
