# Validation plan — gated draft

Not an approved study or experiment. No real participants were contacted. Proposed durations and sample are planning parameters, not observed evidence; the owner/research lead must approve recruitment, consent, and scope before any contact.

## Revision 3 evidence boundary

The separate revision-2 (8 fictional reports) and revision-3 (10 fictional invitees) notes are scenario fixtures, not discovery-study participants. Revision 3 records 2 problem mentions and 8 adequate-routine counterexamples (`E-006`) and remains separate from revision 2 (`E-005`) and the synthetic click table (`E-002`). Neither update changes the gated study design or supplies population prevalence, demand, or success thresholds. No stage is authorized; obtain the human approvals and define criteria stated above before any contact.

## Stage 1 — problem discovery

- **Question:** which users, if any, have a recurring dinner-planning job with a meaningful unmet need, and what do they do today?
- **Method/comparator:** 8–12 purposively recruited exploratory participants, if the owner approves a defensible target-user definition; interview about a recent real planning episode and compare the desired outcome with the current workaround. Include disconfirming cases and accessibility needs. Do not treat concept preference as demand.
- **Primary descriptive measure:** number and share of interviewed participants reporting a recent (past 4 weeks) instance of the defined problem; denominator is all eligible completed interviews, with exclusions reported. This is qualitative discovery, not a population-prevalence estimate.
- **Duration:** up to 2 weeks after approval and recruitment readiness; stop earlier if safety, consent, or scope conditions fail.
- **Decision rule:** product/research owner sets the required recurrence, severity, and feasibility criteria before interviews. Without those criteria, report findings and do not advance automatically to build.
- **Guardrails:** informed consent; no sensitive dietary/health data unless separately approved; no leading concept pitch before the episode discussion; include non-interesting and counterexample cases; stop if scope changes.

## Stage 2 — optional prototype usability test

Only if Stage 1 supports a specific user job and a safe, feasible scope. Compare a narrow opt-in prototype with the participant's current method on the same planning task.

- **Measures:** task completion without help / eligible participants attempting the task; task errors and assistance requests / attempts; participant-reported usefulness as qualitative feedback. Define the task and error coding before sessions.
- **Population/sample:** small purposive usability sample, size set by research owner; do not generalize to market demand.
- **Duration:** up to 2 weeks after prototype and approvals; no paid public release.
- **Threshold:** owner sets minimum task-success and maximum-error criteria against a baseline before the test; values are currently `UNKNOWN` because there is no prototype or baseline.
- **Guardrails:** consent, accessibility coverage, dietary-safety review, no real health advice, and stop on any safety/privacy issue.
- **Next action:** proceed to a separately approved launch business case only if evidence supports the need, prototype clears pre-agreed criteria, and cost/price/operations are validated; otherwise revise or stop.
