# Decision summary — readable without the case folder

- **Decision question:**
- **Decision owner / authority:**
- **Status:** `NOT_MADE | RECOMMENDATION_PENDING_HUMAN | DECIDED`
- **Revision:**
- **Selected direction:** state what is recommended/selected; distinguish recommendation from owner decision.
- **Why:** strongest product-strategy basis and important UNKNOWNs.
- **Communication strategy:** intended audience and choice moment; barrier; message; proof/limitations; channel role. If not validated, say so.
- **Alternatives:** at least three materially different options including status quo and stop/close where applicable; include why rejected and strongest objection.
- **Critical evidence:** source/evidence/claim IDs plus exact file/URL and locator; include metric formula, units and denominator if used.
- **What remains UNKNOWN:** decision-changing gaps only.
- **Validation:** comparator, audience, metric contract, window, guardrails, threshold and approvals; mark missing terms UNKNOWN/not ready.
- **Owner decision:** `NOT PROVIDED | DECIDED`; execution approval, if any, is a separate append-only `approvals.jsonl` record. The ledger is authoritative for current action/scope/revision/hash authorization; an absent/mismatched record means no authorization.
- **Next step:** one concrete action, owner and required approval; no implicit authorization for external action.

Detailed evidence and strategies remain in linked case files; this summary must still communicate the direction, alternatives, product and communication strategy, basis, unknowns, check, owner decision, and next step without requiring the reader to reconstruct the folder.
