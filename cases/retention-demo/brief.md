# Brief — synthetic exercise

**Scenario status:** SYNTHETIC. No actual company, product, user, stakeholder, or measurement is represented.

## Starting request

> «Почему у нас падает удержание, какие изменения надо срочно запустить?»

This is an intentionally ambiguous fictional request. Its premise is not treated as established evidence.

## Decision to support

Should a hypothetical product team change the product, change acquisition, or first improve diagnosis after an apparent two-month retention decline?

## Intake record

- **Decision owner:** UNKNOWN in a real engagement; represented here by a hypothetical product lead. No real person has approved a decision.
- **Audience / user job:** UNKNOWN. The fixture only shows acquisition channel and a synthetic Day-30 event.
- **Desired outcome / deadline:** UNKNOWN. “Urgently” is not a date or success metric.
- **Constraints:** public sources and this synthetic fixture only; no company or customer data; no external action; no user study or experiment authorized.
- **Available material:** `input/cohort-retention.csv`, explicitly fictional; public method references in `sources.jsonl`.
- **Not provided:** metric/event contract, source system, timezone, release history, campaign changes, channel cost/LTV, complaints/support data, decision authority, minimum economically meaningful effect.

## Questions that change the next step

1. Which event and eligible-user definition does the real team use for retention, and is it unchanged across periods?
2. Are cohort periods and the Day-30 window complete and comparable? What timezone and extraction date apply?
3. Did acquisition mix, campaign targeting, product releases, pricing, or instrumentation change?
4. Who owns the decision, what action is reversible, and what time/cost/guardrail limits apply?

Until those answers and real data exist, the synthetic calculation demonstrates a failure mode only; it says nothing about an actual MegaFon product or any real product's cause of churn.
