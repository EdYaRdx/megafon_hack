## Barrier to understanding or choice

R2 adds a claimed capacity/date restriction and a copy request without a supplied audience, channel, approved capability, or matching approval. The remembered R1 sign-off is absent from the approval ledger.

The 8.3-point difference is descriptive; assignment and event rules are unknown. R2 also claims a half-engineer-week cap through 2027-01-15, which must be verified. ## Product capability / condition supporting each promise

No new capability is approved. Do not promise improved retention or imply a prototype exists. Partner-facing claims require validated capability, audience, evidence, and a new approval tied to the exact R2 message, scope, revision and premise.

## Channel role and rationale

Internal decision memo supports bounded decision-readiness work. No external partner channel is selected; list, audience, channel, capability and permission are unknown. Send no partner message, even if the claimed 2026-11-30 request is genuine.

## Claims to withhold or qualify

Withhold “retention win,” “users prefer,” “checklist increases retention,” “we can build/test now,” and demand/ROI claims. Label the CSV synthetic, the return contrast descriptive, the capacity/date statements claimed, and the remembered sign-off unverified and non-authorizing.
