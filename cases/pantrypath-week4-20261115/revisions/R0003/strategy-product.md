# Product strategy (synthetic scenario)

## Audience and job

Fictional weekly meal-planning tool users are a hypothesized audience. Their job, segments, alternatives, and failure moments are `UNKNOWN`; no personas or demand inferred. The R2 partner audience is also `UNKNOWN`.

## Problem evidence and alternatives

`E-001`–`E-003`: synthetic one-week pilot export only. Checklist arm has more recorded week-four returns but less setup completion and more support contacts. Assignment, event definitions, dates, exposure and missingness unknown. Difference is descriptive, not causal. `E-005`–`E-007` add only claimed resource/partner constraints and the verified absence of an approval record.

## Proposed product direction

No feature release, prototype, recruitment, or partner outreach now. Treat the claimed half-engineer-week cap through 2027-01-15 as a constraint pending verification. The interim product step is decision readiness: define the return contract, target segment, first-week task, success threshold, and approval/capacity path without building or contacting users. If the restriction is confirmed to expire and approval is granted, run the low-fidelity paired task diagnostic of current flow vs shorter first-week setup; inspect whether setup burden recurs before choosing onboarding or checklist work. Any later experiment requires an owner-approved event and design.

## Explicitly out of scope

Live recruitment/testing before the claimed restriction expires, deployment, prototype build before verified capacity and approval, partner outreach or copy, retention claims, paid research, real user data, market sizing, revenue forecasts.

## Value, cost and revenue assumptions

Potential value: fewer barriers to completing a first weekly plan (`C-05` hypothesis). No value or revenue quantified. Ops estimates checklist polish at one engineer-week and setup redesign at two (`C-03`); unverified, not approved capacity. R2 claims only half an engineer-week through 2027-01-15 (`C-06`); this is unverified. Research cost and ROI `UNKNOWN`.

## Evidence, hypotheses and unknowns

- Synthetic counts: `E-001`, `E-002`; quality omissions: `E-003`.
- Method guidance: `E-004`, UK Government Digital Service, retrieved 2026-11-15; general research planning only.
- R2 claims and approval check: `E-005`–`E-007`; no approval record exists for R1 or R2.
- Hypothesis `C-05`: setup friction may reduce successful first-week planning. Falsifier: approved diagnostic shows no repeated blocker.
- Unknown: target user, need prevalence, retention contract, causal effect, sample, costs, resource/date validity, partner audience/channel/capability, unit economics and authority.

## Risks and strongest counterargument

Decision-readiness work may consume the limited capacity without affecting week-four behavior. The resource claim may be wrong, checklist may genuinely help, and setup focus could miss that. Counter with explicit premise verification, no unsupported external promise, paired task comparison when feasible, stop rules, and a later controlled test only after metric/data requirements are met.
