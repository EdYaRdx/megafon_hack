# Communication strategy (synthetic scenario)

## Audience and moment

Internal fictional product lead deciding effort on setup, checklist, or promotion. External partner audience and decision moment are `UNKNOWN`.

## Barrier to understanding or choice

“Retention win” has no defined event/window; the small synthetic arm contrast may be misread as causal. No evidence establishes user motivation, partner interest, or business effect.

## Key message and supporting proof

Internal only: “The synthetic pilot recorded 22/48 eligible accounts returning in week four in the checklist arm and 18/48 in current flow. The 8.3-point difference is descriptive; assignment and event rules are unknown, so this is not evidence of lift.” Claims `C-01`–`C-03`; receipt in `test-plan.md`.

## Product capability / condition supporting each promise

No new capability is approved. Do not promise improved retention. Partner-facing claims require validated capability, evidence, audience, and separate approval.

## Channel role and rationale

Decision memo/internal review supports a bounded decision. No external partner channel selected; audience, channel, and permission unknown. Send no partner message.

## Claims to withhold or qualify

Withhold “retention win,” “users prefer,” “checklist increases retention,” and demand/ROI claims. Label the CSV synthetic and return contrast descriptive.
