# Product strategy (synthetic scenario)

## Audience and job

Fictional weekly meal-planning tool users are a hypothesized audience. Their job, segments, alternatives, and failure moments are `UNKNOWN`; no personas or demand inferred.

## Problem evidence and alternatives

`E-001`–`E-003`: synthetic one-week pilot export only. Checklist arm has more recorded week-four returns but less setup completion and more support contacts. Assignment, event definitions, dates, exposure and missingness unknown. Difference is descriptive, not causal.

## Proposed product direction

No feature release yet. After approval, run a low-fidelity paired task diagnostic of current flow vs shorter first-week setup; inspect whether setup burden recurs before choosing onboarding or checklist work. If no actionable friction appears, preserve status quo or close. Any later experiment requires an owner-approved event and design.

## Explicitly out of scope

Live recruitment/testing, deployment, partner outreach, retention claims, paid research, real user data, market sizing, revenue forecasts.

## Value, cost and revenue assumptions

Potential value: fewer barriers to completing a first weekly plan (`C-05` hypothesis). No value or revenue quantified. Ops estimates checklist polish at one engineer-week and setup redesign at two (`C-03`); unverified, not approved capacity. Research cost and ROI `UNKNOWN`.

## Evidence, hypotheses and unknowns

- Synthetic counts: `E-001`, `E-002`; quality omissions: `E-003`.
- Method guidance: `E-004`, UK Government Digital Service, retrieved 2026-11-15; general research planning only.
- Hypothesis `C-05`: setup friction may reduce successful first-week planning. Falsifier: approved diagnostic shows no repeated blocker.
- Unknown: target user, need prevalence, retention contract, causal effect, sample, costs, partner channel, unit economics.

## Risks and strongest counterargument

Setup diagnostics may consume time without affecting week-four behavior. Checklist may genuinely help, and setup focus could miss that. Counter this with paired task comparison, explicit stop rules, and a later controlled test only after metric/data requirements are met.
